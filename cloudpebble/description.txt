TITLE

Nexon


TAGLINE

A cushion-shaped analogue dial in the spirit of 1970s German watchmaking.


DESCRIPTION

Nexon is a clean analogue watchface with applied baton indices, an inner minute track and a date window at six. The dial carries the fictional maker's mark Nexon Klarwerk.

On Pebble Time 2 the dial follows the rectangular screen with a squared minute track. On Pebble Round 2 the track and indices are round and the hands are longer to use the full dial.


SETTINGS

- Colour - turquoise, white with a navy minute ring, or navy.
- Second hand - on or off. With the second hand off the face only redraws once a minute and saves battery.


CATEGORY

Watchfaces
